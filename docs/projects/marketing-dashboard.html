Marketing dashboard

The firm's first marketing dashboard in Power BI, following visitors to leads to paying clients in one view.

My role
Built it as part of the analytics team
Where
City Traders Imperium
Tools
Power BI, Power Query, DAX, Google Analytics data

Problem

Marketing relied only on Google Analytics. Ad spend, website traffic, leads and payments sat in separate places, so there was no single view of what a client cost or where paying clients came from.

What I built

The firm's first marketing dashboard in Power BI, built as part of the analytics team:

  • An overview with lead cost, client cost and revenue against their targets, and the funnel from visitor to paying client
  • Leads by country, source and month, with the lead conversion rate
  • Clients and revenue against ad spend, month by month
  • Coupon use on first purchase

What it makes possible

  • Move ad budget to the countries and sources that bring paying clients, not just leads
  • See each month when the cost per client goes above target
  • Check whether coupons bring in new clients

This is work for my employer, so the details here are kept general. I'm happy to go into more depth in an interview.
