Group trading detection

A scored evidence report on any group of traders, ready in about 3 minutes.

My role
Designed and built it
Where
City Traders Imperium
Tools
Python, SQL, Excel reports

Problem

Some traders coordinate across separate accounts. Proving it meant comparing trade histories by hand, which took a long time and got harder with every trader added.

What I built

A tool that compares the trading activity of any group of traders, scores how closely each pair is connected and exports the evidence as a report for case review.

Result

3 minutes

A full report is ready in about 3 minutes, even for groups of 10 or 20 traders.

This is work for my employer, so the details here are kept general. I'm happy to go into more depth in an interview.
