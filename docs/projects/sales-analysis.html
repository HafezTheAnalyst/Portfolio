Sales analysis, answered in six tools

A year of sales spread over 12 CSV files, cleaned, combined and analysed in six different tools to compare how each one handles the job.

Type
Personal project
Data
12 monthly CSV files
Tools
Excel, SQL, R, Python, Tableau, Power BI

The question

When do sales peak, which products sell best, and what should the business do about advertising, promotion and stock?

What I did

  • Excel: combined, cleaned and charted the 12 files
  • SQL: queried the combined data for the detailed analysis
  • R: cleaned and reshaped the data and wrote the report in R Markdown
  • Python: explored the data with Pandas, NumPy and Matplotlib
  • Tableau and Power BI: built interactive dashboards

What I found

  • The peak sales months and the best-selling products
  • When customers buy, and which products they buy together
  • Recommendations for ad timing, product promotion and stock levels
