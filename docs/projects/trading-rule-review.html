Automated trading-rule review

A review that took about 20 minutes per account now takes about 2, and checks far more than the manual review did.

My role
Designed and built it
Where
City Traders Imperium
Tools
Python, SQL, Excel reports

Problem

Every account has to be reviewed against the firm's trading rules before it moves forward. By hand this took about 20 minutes per account and covered only the basic checks, so violations were sometimes missed.

What I built

A tool that analyses an account's full trade history and checks it against each rule automatically: risk per trade, risk per trade idea, hedging, copy trading and the firm's other trading rules.

When the firm adds a new rule, I add it as a new automated check.

Result

90% faster

A review that took about 20 minutes now takes about 2, and up to 5 for the largest accounts. Every account is checked against the same rules, and the review covers far more than the manual one did.
