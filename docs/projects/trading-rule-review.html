Automated trading-rule review

A review that took about 20 minutes per account now takes about 2, and checks far more than the manual review did.

My role
Designed and built it
Where
City Traders Imperium
Tools
Python, SQL, Excel reports

Problem

Every account has to be reviewed against the firm's trading rules before it moves forward. By hand this took about 20 minutes per account and covered only the basic checks, so violations were sometimes missed.

What I built

A tool that analyses an account's full trade history and checks it against each rule automatically. It covers the rules prop firms care about most: risk per trade and per trade idea, martingale, hedging, copy trading, one-sided trading and margin usage, along with the firm's other trading rules.

For each rule it shows the trades that broke it and the profit made on them. It also builds a performance scorecard for the account: net profit, best and worst trade, largest floating drawdown, longest winning and losing runs, and highest risk exposure.

When the firm adds a new rule, I add it as a new automated check.

Result

90% faster

A review that took about 20 minutes now takes about 2, and up to 5 for the largest accounts. Every account is checked against the same rules, and the review covers far more than the manual one did.

This is work for my employer, so the details here are kept general. I'm happy to go into more depth in an interview.
