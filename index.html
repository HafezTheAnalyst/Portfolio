Two kinds of work, one habit of mind

Data analysis

Taking a business question, finding the data that answers it and presenting the answer so someone can act on it.

  • Cleaning and combining messy sources in Python, R and Excel
  • Analysis in SQL on PostgreSQL and ClickHouse
  • Dashboards and data stories in Tableau and Power BI
  • Reports that end with a recommendation, not just a chart

Risk and trading operations

Watching how traders behave across MT5 and MatchTrader accounts and catching the patterns that break the rules.

  • Monitoring trading activity against firm rules
  • Detecting copy trading, hedging between accounts and shared devices or IPs
  • Automating checks and weekly management reporting
  • Leading a back-office team through payouts and account operations

My employer's tools and data are confidential, so the projects below are personal and course work. I'm happy to walk through the risk work in an interview.

Projects

Sales analysis, answered in six tools

A year of sales spread over 12 CSV files. I cleaned and combined them, then ran the same analysis in Excel, SQL, R, Python, Tableau and Power BI to compare how each tool handles the job.

  • Found the peak sales months and the best-selling products
  • Mapped when and what customers buy together
  • Recommended ad timing, product promotion and stock levels
View the code on GitHub

What drives infant mortality in high-GDP countries

A Tableau data story covering the three highest-GDP countries in each region from 2000 to 2012. It compares infant mortality with birth rate, health spending per person, health spending as a share of GDP and population.

  • Built as a guided story, so a reader follows the argument page by page
  • Filters set up for healthcare, public health and general readers
Open the story on Tableau Public

Top-rated businesses in the Yelp dataset

A SQL project on the Yelp dataset to find the top-rated restaurants, shops, and beauty and spa businesses across several states.

  • Joined several tables and checked the results for accuracy
  • Compared businesses by rating, location and whether they are still open
  • Verified a sample of results against the businesses' own listings
